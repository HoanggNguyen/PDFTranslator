\subsection{Cell Geometry: Sampling and Sensitivity}
\label{app:cells}

The sample is 1{,}499 of the 93{,}834 tables in the PubTables-1M test split, drawn with a
fixed seed and stratified by presence of a spanning cell and by cell count. The proportion
of tables containing a spanning cell is $52.8\%$ in the sample against $52.7\%$ in the
population; the median table has 50 cells.

Table~\ref{tab:app-res-cells} varies the three choices that could otherwise be read as
tuning. The confidence threshold is inert: $F_1$ moves by $0.013$ across the range, with
precision and recall trading off as expected. The non-overlap filter is not inert: it
costs $0.117$ recall on spanning cells while leaving $F_1$ unchanged, which is why it is
reported in the main text as a trade-off rather than an oversight. The table region
matters most---measured on the padded source image instead of the annotated region, $F_1$
falls to $0.785$ and $F_1$ at the strict threshold to $0.459$, which bounds how much the
cell stage depends on the quality of the box it is given.
