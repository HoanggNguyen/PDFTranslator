Please consider our Original Research article, “Layout-Preserving Multilingual
Translation of Documents in Portable Document Format with Large Language
Models,” for publication in *Engineering Applications of Artificial
Intelligence*.

The article addresses an engineering problem in which machine translation and
document reconstruction must be solved jointly. Its artificial-intelligence
contribution is a visually grounded, identifier-preserving pipeline that
separates document structure analysis, multilingual translation, and
geometry-aware reconstruction. Its engineering application is the automatic
translation of scientific and technical documents while preserving tables,
mathematical expressions, reading order, and page appearance.

The study evaluates the stages independently on public benchmarks: 1,651
OmniDocBench pages, 1,499 PubTables-1M tables, and all 55 target languages in
WMT24++. It also reports an end-to-end comparison on 204 pages. The results show
which structure-analysis errors are unrecoverable downstream and demonstrate
that reconstruction is more reliable than prompt-level character-length
constraints for preserving layout.
